New package: usage credits extracted from Lore Spawner's lore tokens. Ships SQL and Hasura metadata templates (`hasura/`, rendered by `kk-hasura sync`) for a priced usage ledger, balances, a monthly dollar-funded free pool, admin grants, Stripe purchases with auto-refill and a user's own model key, all row-locked and server-only; `createCredits` (`admit`, `beforeCall`, `onUsage`) and a store over an injected executor; pure pricing, pool, billing and grant rules; Stripe purchases (`/stripe`), price feeds (`/feeds`) and screens on `@kaperkunde/ui` (`/ui`).
